What you see

A TRS-80 Model 4 (like the 4P), emulated in your browser: the Z80, 128K of banked memory, the screen of 80×24 or 64×16 characters, the keyboard, the clock interrupt 30 times a second and the WD1010 hard disk controller. Like a 4P or a Model 4 with a FreHD, it starts from the hard disk, or from floppy 1. Choose the system under Hard disk; the images are .hdv files as FreHD, xtrs and trs80gp use them.

There are two 5¼" floppy drives on an FD1793 controller. For each drive, choose a floppy, a blank (unformatted) floppy or your own image (.dsk in JV3 or JV1 format, .dmk or .imd); you can swap them while it runs. What is written to them stays in your browser; Save as downloads the floppy as .dsk (JV3), .dmk or .imd.

Click the screen to type on your own keyboard, or use the buttons and the line above (handy on a phone). Esc is BREAK, Home is CLEAR, F1-F3 are F1-F3 and Ctrl works as CTRL.

Fuzix is a small Unix-like system. Log in as root; Ctrl-C interrupts a program. To start from one of the Fuzix boot floppies, choose floppy 1 under Boot from; the file system is then on the hard disk too: if Fuzix asks at bootdev:, answer hda2. Floppy 1 and 2 are /dev/fd0 and /dev/fd1. Fuzix cannot format a floppy, but it can use the LS-DOS data floppy (or one formatted by LS-DOS): mkfs /dev/fd0 32 360 makes a file system on it, and mount /dev/fd0 /mnt puts it under /mnt. Stop with shutdown.

LS-DOS 6.3.1 is the operating system by Logical Systems and MISOSYS, adapted here to start from the hard disk (drive :0). It asks for the date and time at every start (MM/DD/YY and HH:MM:SS); the buttons fill in the current ones. Then you can give commands such as DIR, FREE, COPY, LIST and LIB (all built-in commands). Floppy 1 and 2 are drives :1 and :2: FORMAT :1 formats a blank floppy, after which COPY, DIR :1 and BACKUP work.

Changes to the hard disk last until you reload the page or restart; floppies stay in their drives when you restart. Z80 emulation: z80-emulator by Lawrence Kesteloot (MIT). LS-DOS: © MISOSYS, free to distribute with permission from Roy Soltoff (see NOTICE/TXT on the disk).
